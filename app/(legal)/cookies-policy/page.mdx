# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This page explains which ones we use and how you can control them.

## How consent works here

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept optional cookies. Nothing optional loads until you choose **Accept All**. If you choose **Reject**, only the essential items below are used. Your choice is saved in your browser so we do not ask again.

## Essential

These are needed for the site to work and cannot be switched off.

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| syntechire-consent | Remembers your cookie choice (kept in your browser's local storage) | Until you clear site data |

## Optional

These load only with your consent.

| Tool | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google Ads tag | Google LLC | Measures whether visitors who arrived from our Google ads went on to send an enquiry. It sets Google advertising cookies, such as `_gcl_au`, and Google's own cookie policy applies. |

We do not use analytics or chat tools. The WhatsApp button is a plain link: it sets no cookies on this site, and WhatsApp's own policy applies once you open it.

If we add another optional tool, we will list it here and it will load only after you choose **Accept All**.

## Changing your choice

Clear this site's data in your browser settings and reload the page. The banner will appear again.
