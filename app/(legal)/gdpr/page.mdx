## Our role

**As a controller.** For information collected through this website, such as enquiries and newsletter subscriptions, SyntaxHires decides how and why the data is used.

**As a processor.** When our engineers work on a client's systems, the client is the controller of any personal data in those systems. We act on the client's instructions under a data processing agreement.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Have inaccurate data corrected
- Have data erased in certain circumstances
- Restrict or object to processing
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
- Complain to your data protection authority
